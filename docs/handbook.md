## 1. About the Restaurant

The Copper Spoon Bistro is a casual dining restaurant serving
Indian-inspired dishes and international favourites. It has 72 indoor
seats and 16 patio seats. The restaurant operates every day.

**Guest operating hours**

  Service     Days                  Hours
  ----------- --------------------- -------------------
  Breakfast   Saturday and Sunday   8:00 AM--11:00 AM
  Lunch       Monday--Sunday        12:00 PM--3:30 PM
  Dinner      Monday--Sunday        6:30 PM--10:30 PM

The restaurant is closed on 26 January, 1 May, and 25 December.
Additional closure dates are announced by management.

Our service principles are hospitality, consistency, cleanliness,
safety, and respect. Every employee is responsible for treating guests
and colleagues professionally.

## 2. Roles and Responsibilities

-   **General Manager (GM):** Overall operations, staffing decisions,
    serious complaints, and policy exceptions.
-   **Assistant Manager (AM):** Shift supervision, cash reconciliation,
    roster coordination, and daily reports.
-   **Head Chef:** Kitchen operations, recipes, food quality, and
    food-safety procedures.
-   **Sous Chef:** Kitchen supervision, stock checks, and preparation
    coordination.
-   **Floor Supervisor:** Guest service, reservations, table allocation,
    and service recovery.
-   **People & Operations Coordinator:** Employee records, attendance,
    leave, onboarding, and payroll queries.

Employees should first contact their immediate supervisor. If the
concern involves that supervisor, they may contact the General Manager
or People & Operations Coordinator.

## 3. Employment and Onboarding

Full-time employees normally work 40 hours per week. Part-time, trainee,
and fixed-term schedules are defined in their appointment terms.

New full-time employees normally have a 90-calendar-day probation
period. A formal review is usually conducted around day 75. Management
may confirm employment or extend probation once for up to 60 calendar
days, with written communication.

New employees must submit their identity and address documents, PAN
details where applicable, bank account information, emergency contact,
photograph, and completed employee form within five working days of
joining.

All new employees complete a two-day orientation covering workplace
conduct, emergency exits, attendance, hygiene, and role-specific
procedures. Employees must not independently operate equipment until
trained and approved.

## 4. Shifts and Attendance

The weekly roster is normally published by Thursday at 6:00 PM for the
following Monday-to-Sunday week.

  Shift                           Scheduled time
  ------------------------------- -------------------
  B1 --- Weekend breakfast        7:30 AM--4:00 PM
  L1 --- Lunch                    10:30 AM--7:00 PM
  D1 --- Dinner                   2:30 PM--11:00 PM
  M1 --- Management/preparation   9:00 AM--5:30 PM

A standard full shift includes a 30-minute unpaid meal break and two
paid 10-minute rest breaks, subject to coverage and applicable
requirements. Employees must follow their published roster.

Attendance is recorded using the digital terminal at the staff entrance.
Employees must never clock in or out for another person. A missed punch
must be reported to the supervisor before the end of the shift.

An employee is considered late when they reach their assigned station
more than five minutes after the scheduled start. Three late arrivals in
one calendar month trigger a documented coaching discussion. Six may
trigger a formal attendance review.

Employees who cannot attend because of illness or an emergency should
call the duty manager at least two hours before the shift whenever
possible. Shift swaps require agreement from both employees and written
supervisor approval before the affected shift.

## 5. Leave Policy

The leave year runs from 1 January to 31 December. Eligible full-time
employees receive:

  Leave type                                   Annual entitlement
  ------------------- -------------------------------------------
  Casual leave                                             8 days
  Sick leave                                               8 days
  Earned leave                                            12 days
  Bereavement leave     Up to 3 working days per qualifying event

Casual leave requests should normally be submitted two days in advance
for one day off, or seven days in advance for two or more consecutive
days. Unused casual leave expires at year-end.

Employees should notify the duty manager about sickness at least two
hours before the shift where possible. A medical certificate may be
requested for more than two consecutive scheduled working days of
sickness absence.

Earned leave should normally be requested at least 14 calendar days in
advance. A maximum of six consecutive earned-leave days may normally be
approved without additional General Manager approval. Earned leave can
be carried forward up to a maximum balance of 18 days.

Planned leave is restricted during 20 December--2 January and 10--15
August, except where the General Manager approves an exception. These
restrictions do not prevent employees from reporting illness or
requesting emergency leave.

A leave request is not approved until written confirmation is received.
Employees must not assume that a pending request has been accepted.

## 6. Uniform and Hygiene

Service employees wear a black shirt, charcoal trousers, black
closed-toe shoes, apron, and name badge. Kitchen employees wear a chef
coat, kitchen trousers, apron, non-slip shoes, and hair restraint.

Uniforms must be clean at the beginning of every shift. Hair must be
secured away from the face. Fingernails must be short and clean.
Artificial nails and nail polish are not permitted for employees
handling unpackaged food.

Employees must wash hands before food preparation, after using the
restroom, after handling raw meat or waste, after touching phones or
money, and whenever changing from a contaminated task to a clean task.
Gloves do not replace handwashing.

Personal bags and phones must be kept in the designated staff area.
Personal phone use is allowed during breaks or for an approved
operational task.

## 7. Food Safety

The restaurant uses these internal temperature targets:

  Storage or process                           Target
  -------------------- ------------------------------
  Chiller                                    0°C--5°C
  Freezer                             -18°C or colder
  Hot holding                           63°C or above
  Poultry cooking        75°C for at least 30 seconds

Chiller and freezer temperatures are recorded twice daily, at 9:00 AM
and 4:00 PM. If the chiller is above 5°C, the employee must notify the
Sous Chef immediately. The Sous Chef assesses the food, duration, and
equipment before deciding whether products should be transferred or
discarded.

All prepared or opened food stored for later use must be labelled with
its name, preparation or opening date and time, use-by information, and
employee initials. The kitchen follows first-expiry-first-out (FEFO)
stock rotation.

Raw meat, poultry, seafood, and eggs must be stored and prepared
separately from ready-to-eat food. Use the colour-coded cutting boards
displayed beside the preparation sink. Clean and sanitize equipment
between incompatible tasks.

Employees must report vomiting, diarrhoea, or other symptoms that could
affect safe food handling. Unsafe food must never be served to reduce
waste.

### Allergies

If a guest reports an allergy, the service employee must record it in
the POS and inform both the Floor Supervisor and Head Chef. The kitchen
checks the current allergen matrix and follows the allergy preparation
procedure. Employees must never promise that a dish is allergen-free if
cross-contact cannot be controlled. If safe preparation cannot be
verified, the restaurant must explain this to the guest.

## 8. Guest Service and Reservations

Guests should be acknowledged within 30 seconds whenever possible.
Employees must repeat orders before submitting them to the POS.

Normal service targets are: - Water: within 3 minutes. - Beverages:
within 7 minutes. - Starters: within 15 minutes. - Main courses: within
25 minutes. - Bill after request: within 5 minutes.

These are targets, not guarantees. Employees should communicate
realistic delays.

Reservations are held for 15 minutes after the booked time. A party
arriving later may be moved to the next available table. Reservations
for eight or more guests require a 20% deposit. Groups of 12 or more
require General Manager approval.

Employees should handle complaints using CARE: Connect with the guest,
Acknowledge the concern, Resolve it within their authority, and Escalate
when necessary. A service employee may replace an incorrect dish or
offer a non-alcoholic beverage up to ₹250 with supervisor approval.
Discounts above 10% require General Manager approval.

## 9. Cash, Payments, and Staff Benefits

The restaurant accepts cash, cards, UPI through its approved merchant QR
code, and authorized gift vouchers. Employees must never use a personal
UPI ID to collect restaurant payments.

The standard opening cashier float is ₹5,000. Cashiers print the POS
shift summary, count physical cash, record any shortage or overage, and
obtain Assistant Manager verification. Employees must not alter POS
records or personally replace a cash shortage.

Employees working at least six hours receive one staff meal. Eligible
employees receive a 20% discount on personal food purchases outside
their scheduled shift, for a maximum of two guests including the
employee. Alcoholic beverages, gift vouchers, and delivery orders are
excluded.

Voluntary tips are pooled and distributed monthly: 45% to the service
team, 35% to the kitchen team, 10% to stewarding, and 10% to eligible
shift support and runners.

## 10. Safety, Conduct, and Emergencies

Harassment, discrimination, bullying, violence, theft, retaliation, and
deliberate falsification of records are prohibited. Employees should
report concerns to the General Manager or People & Operations
Coordinator. Concerns will be handled as confidentially as reasonably
possible.

Employees must report injuries, burns, cuts, spills, damaged equipment,
pest activity, and food contamination. Only trained employees may
operate commercial mixers, meat slicers, deep fryers, and other
restricted equipment.

For a fire, raise the alarm, alert the manager, evacuate using the
nearest safe exit, and assemble in the open parking area across
Riverside Market Road. Do not re-enter until authorized. The national
emergency number in India is **112**. The first-aid kit is in the staff
corridor beside the office.

Smoking and vaping are prohibited inside the restaurant, kitchen,
storage rooms, staff dining area, and guest patio. Employees must not
report for duty impaired by alcohol or drugs.

## 11. Payroll and Resignation

The payroll period runs from the first to the last calendar day of each
month. Salary is normally transferred on the last working day of the
month. Employees should report payslip discrepancies to People &
Operations within seven calendar days.

The standard notice period is 30 calendar days for confirmed full-time
employees and 15 calendar days for employees on probation. Part-time
employees normally provide 15 calendar days, subject to their
appointment terms.

Resignations must be submitted in writing to the reporting manager with
a copy to People & Operations. Before leaving, employees must return
restaurant property, complete handover duties, settle approved cash or
stock responsibilities, and confirm their contact details. Final
settlement and employment documents are processed after required
clearances and in accordance with applicable requirements.
